Cartridge Heater

A high-density cylindrical heating element designed to be inserted directly into drilled holes in metal dies, platens, moulds, and machine components. Cartridge heaters deliver precise and localized heat exactly where it is needed, making them ideal for injection moulding, die casting, and packaging machinery.

  • High watt density for precise localized heating
  • Inserted directly into drilled holes in metal components
  • Available in single-ended and double-ended configurations
  • Stainless steel sheath for corrosion resistance
  • Custom diameters, lengths and wattage available
  • Suitable for temperatures up to 750°C
  • Optional thermocouple for temperature monitoring
